Pit display

Your next match and the rankings, on the screen in your pit

One full-screen page: the next match with both alliances, your rank and record, your matches and the event's live rankings. It finds your event by itself, keeps the screen awake, and keeps showing the last data when the venue's wifi drops. Set it up here, then open the link on the TV.

Open the display
  • Open the link in the browser of the TV or laptop in your pit and press F for full screen. Bookmark it to start straight away next time.
  • During the event it refreshes every 30 seconds; before it starts it checks every 5 minutes, so it can be set up the evening before.
  • The screen stays on while the page is open, where the browser allows it (the footer says so).
