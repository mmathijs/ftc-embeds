Pit display

Your next match and the rankings, on the screen in your pit

One full-screen page: the next match with both alliances, your rank and record, your matches and the event's live rankings. It finds your event by itself, keeps the screen awake, and keeps showing the last data when the venue's wifi drops.

01Team

Its current event is found by itself. For another one, add &event=NLCMP to the address.

02Screen
Then press F for full screen.
  • Open it in the browser of the TV or laptop in your pit. Bookmark the page it opens (ftc-embeds.com/display?team=…) to start straight away next time.
  • During the event it refreshes every 30 seconds; before it starts it checks every 5 minutes, so it can be set up the evening before.
  • The screen stays on while the page is open, where the browser allows it (the footer says so).
