Pit display

Your next match and the rankings, on the TV in your pit

One full-screen page with the team's next match, its matches and the event's live rankings. It finds the event by itself, keeps the screen awake, and keeps showing the last data when the venue's wifi drops.

  • Open it in the browser of the TV or laptop, then press F (or the button) for full screen.
  • Bookmark the page it opens: ftc-embeds.com/display?team=… starts straight away.
  • During the event it refreshes every 30 seconds. With an event parameter (&event=NLCMP) it shows that event instead.
