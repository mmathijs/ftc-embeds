What you get

Up to eight options, each as a PNG with a background and a transparent PNG:

  • the whole logo, with its background or on the colour with the most contrast;
  • the main part only, without a separate name line;
  • the top part, cut off at the bottom edge, for tall mascots;
  • a coin with a complete ring, for round badges;
  • crisper colours, for soft or photo-like logos.

Not quite right? Every option has an Edit button: an editor with an eraser, a restore brush, a magic wand for leftover gaps, a brush, a pen for curves and shapes, with the 40 px result shown live.

Each option is checked against FIRST's rules (40 × 40 px, at most 20 kB, no colours near lime or magenta, which video overlays key out) and shown at actual size on FIRST's backgrounds. FTCScout shows avatars at 40 and 20 px.

Upload it to FIRST

  1. Lead Mentor 1 or 2 logs in at ftc-scoring.firstinspires.org.
  2. Choose Team Avatar, then Upload New Avatar, and pick one PNG.
  3. Upload at least 2 weeks before your event. Changes can take up to 24 hours to show.
  4. One submission at a time, and FIRST may not review more than three a season. See FIRST's avatar rules.
