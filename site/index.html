For teams

Your FIRST avatar, made from your logo

Upload your logo and get 40 × 40 avatars that follow FIRST's rules, shown the way FTCScout and the event screens show them. Then make it yours in the editor.

  • The background and the gaps inside letters are removed for you
  • Options on teal, navy and black, without the text, as a coin
  • An editor with layers, an eraser, a magic wand and a pen for curves
  • Saved in your browser; export PNG, SVG, ICO and bigger sizes

      iframe, HTML element or npm package · works with any website builder · no account, no cookies · data from FTCScout and FIRST

      Each widget

      Every widget comes as an iframe, an HTML element and an npm package, with light, dark, FTCScout, FTC Events and trophy-case themes, shapes, and your own colors, corners, texts, number and date formats and links.

      For events

      Pit map

      Your event's pit map on your own page, ready for visitors on their phones.

      • Search a team and get a walking route to its pit, the fields or queueing
      • Highlight and outline teams in your own colors
      • Load it from FTC Events, or upload the saved page
      Configure →<ftc-pit-map>
      For teams

      Team card

      Your team at a glance, for the top of your team's website.

      • Pick the stats: OPR, EPA, rank in your region, records, awards and more
      • Your next events with a countdown
      • A full card, or one compact row
      Configure →<ftc-team-card>
      For teams

      Season results

      Every event of a season with how it went.

      • Rank and record per event
      • Awards and advancement as labels
      • A list, or a strip of tiles that scrolls sideways
      Configure →<ftc-team-results>
      For teams

      Awards

      Every award your team has won, from the first season on.

      • Grouped by season
      • A list, trophy cards, or FTC-style banners
      • Only the awards won, or only some types
      Configure →<ftc-team-awards>
      For teams

      Next match

      While you compete: what's next, updating by itself.

      • Your next match, its time and a countdown
      • Both alliances, your last result and your rank
      • Between events: your next event
      Configure →<ftc-next-match>
      For teams

      Season + next match

      Season results with the live match on top during events.

      • One widget for a team homepage
      • Live part only shows while you compete
      • Same options as both widgets
      Configure →<ftc-team-season>
      For teams and events

      Countdown

      Days, hours and minutes to an event, or to your team's next match.

      • To the first day, or the first match
      • "Happening now" with the livestream while it's on
      • Boxes, or one line
      Configure →<ftc-countdown>
      For events and teams

      Event rankings

      An event's rankings, live during the event.

      • Choose the columns: RP, record, win %, OPR, EPA, …
      • Divisions to switch between
      • For a team: its current event, or a tab per event
      Configure →<ftc-event-rankings>
      For events

      Match schedule

      Every match with its time, alliances and score.

      • The next match marked, updating by itself
      • Quals or playoffs, played or to play
      • One team's own schedule
      Configure →<ftc-event-matches>
      For events

      Advancement

      Who advances from an event, and how.

      • The slots and where they lead
      • Each team's points: qualification, alliance, playoff, award
      • Who advanced, and who couldn't
      Configure →<ftc-event-advancement>
      For leagues

      League standings

      A league's standings, current all season.

      • From any of its meets, or its tournament
      • Rank, average RP and record
      • Your teams highlighted
      Configure →<ftc-league-standings>
